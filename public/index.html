ARMS

A local-first, open-source gateway that gives your AI agents real tools — starting with a native Gmail connector: read, search, organize, draft, and send, all approval-gated and running on your own machine or your own cloud accounts.

  • Bring your own Google OAuth. No shared app, no pooled quota, no third party in your mail path.
  • Approval-gated by design. Reads run freely; every send, draft, label change, archive, and trash waits for your explicit yes.
  • Your data stays yours. Encrypted local vault plus databases you own (MongoDB Atlas / Aiven). Nothing phones home.
